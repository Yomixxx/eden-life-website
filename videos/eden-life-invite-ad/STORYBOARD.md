## Video direction

- **Palette** (from `frame.md`): field/canvas = `ink` (#080f0e) and `pink` (#111f1d) for secondary panels; accent = `pink-deep` (#5ec957, the site's real brand green) used sparingly on underlines/lockup/CTA; text = `cream` (#ffffff) high-emphasis, `cream-2` (#C9E1DE) muted. Never introduce a new hue.
- **Type**: Montserrat display for the wordmark/headline; Poppins body for supporting lines.
- **Motion grammar**: `power3` long-tail everywhere. This is documentary photography, not UI — a slow continuous push/pan IS allowed on the photographic beats (Frames 1, 3, 4), but each push is ONE continuous move that eases to a stop before the back ~30% of its frame, never a re-push or drift in the back half.
- **Rhythm**: Frames 2 and 5 are the deliberate held/breather beats (title-card and logo lockup) bracketing the busier photographic frames 1, 3, 4.
- **Negative list**: no bouncy/elastic entrances, no lazy breathing loops, no stock "AI gradient" bokeh, no on-screen UI chrome — this is a photographic piece about real people, not software.
