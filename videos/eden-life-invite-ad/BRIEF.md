## Intent

A 30-second horizontal hero video for the Eden Life Church homepage —
general brand/invite ad, not tied to any single event. Warm "belong here"
angle: lean on the church's own community/"new here"/testimonies
positioning rather than a hard sell on doctrine or a specific program. Close
on a plan-your-visit CTA. Tone: welcoming, warm, unhurried — this plays as
the site's own hero video, so it should feel at home embedded there.
