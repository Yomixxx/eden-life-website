## Intent

Second creative variant of the full-site walkthrough ad (v1 lives in
`videos/eden-life-site-tour/`). v1 toured the site in its own nav order
(Home → New Here → Who We Are → Grow → Connect → Watch → Give → Events →
Testimonies → CTA) with page-name caption labels — an informational
directory tour. The user asked for a genuinely different creative angle
for a second version, same subject.

**v2's angle**: tell it as a lived, chronological story of a first-time
visitor's actual Sunday, in second person ("you"), rather than a menu of
site sections. Reorders the same real content into an experiential arc
instead of the site's own navigation order:

hook (imagine your first Sunday) → arrival/practical (New Here) → the
room (Home hero / real worship) → the teaching (Watch/Sermons) → you
don't leave alone (Connect) → there's a next step (Grow) → it's already
happening (Events, God Moment tie-in) → giving is easy, no pressure
(Give) → it's already changing lives (Testimonies) → CTA (come see your
first Sunday for yourself).

Caption cards drop the page-name labels ("New Here", "Grow", "Connect")
in favor of short narrative lines in the same second-person voice
("You arrive.", "You're taught, not talked at.", "You don't leave
alone.") so it reads as one continuous story, not a site directory.
