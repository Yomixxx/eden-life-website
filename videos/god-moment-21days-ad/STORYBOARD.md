## Video direction

- **Palette** (from `frame.md`): field = `black` (#080f0e); primary accent = `coral` (#f4de3a, brand-mapped yellow) for numerals/highlights; secondary accent = `coral-dark` (#c81e2b, brand-mapped red) for the GOD MOMENT wordmark; text = `cream`/`white`. Never introduce a new hue — the flyer's own red/yellow already live here as accents.
- **Type**: Montserrat display (uppercase, tracked) for numerals/wordmark; Poppins body for schedule/CTA fine print.
- **Motion grammar**: `power3` long-tail settles everywhere; no bounce/overshoot. Reveal each element on its VO cue.
- **Rhythm**: Frame 1 is a fast two-beat punch (no hold — it's 4s of pure motion). Frame 2 develops across its full 6s as the VO names each element. Frame 3 is the deliberate held/breather close — low motion is the payload once the lockup lands.
- **Negative list**: no bouncy/elastic entrances, no lazy breathing/idle drift, no camera pan in the back half, no confetti or celebratory effects (reverent, not festive), no stock "AI gradient" bokeh.
